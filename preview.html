Before you place a request

Supply, with
clear answers.

No live-stock guesses. No surprise substitutions. We confirm the details before you approve an order.

Can you arrange products that are not shown in the catalogue?

Yes. Share the product name, manufacturer, catalogue code or a photo reference, plus the quantity and delivery pincode. We can check on-demand sourcing and confirm whether it can be arranged.

How do I know what I am being quoted for?

Ask for the manufacturer, exact product/variant, pack size, quantity, price and estimated dispatch or delivery timeline. Available batch, expiry, invoice and product documentation details are shared where applicable.

What happens if the requested item is unavailable?

We will tell you that it is unavailable or requires additional sourcing time. Any alternate brand, model, size or pack must be shown to you and approved before proceeding.

Do you show live stock or guarantee same-day delivery?

No. This website is an enquiry catalogue, not a live inventory system. Availability and delivery feasibility are checked for each order before confirmation.
